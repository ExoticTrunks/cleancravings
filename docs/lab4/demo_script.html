1. Plan and roles

We present CleanCravings as a prototype to a client choosing which product to invest in: show that it meets the client's requirements, that it is built on real government data, and that its design can grow. The demo follows the scenarios of two end users, a guest who has not signed in and a signed-in diner with a health condition, then covers SE practices and traceability with slides.

2. Before the demo

The day before

  • Run the full data sync so the app shows live data, not the "Sample data" banner: in backend/, python -m etl.run_all (drop the latest SFA export into etl/inbox/ first if you have one).
  • Check the Supabase project is active (free projects pause after a week without use) and open it once.
  • Rehearse the whole run sheet with a timer at least once, on the demo laptop.
  • Prepare the demo account: a Google account the team can use on stage. Sign in once and leave its health profile empty, so part B can show the profile setup. Delete its old bookmarks and visits if it was used in rehearsal.
  • Prepare a backup browser profile that is already signed in with a completed profile (see section 5).
  • Have the slides (CleanCravings: SE Practices & Traceability) and the IDE ready, with these files open in tabs: hygiene_controller.py, badges.tsx, classification_rules.csv, rules/engine.py, healthier_eating_controller.py, labels.ts, tests/test_lab4.py.

One hour before

  • Start the backend (uvicorn app.main:app) and the frontend (npm run dev). Wait about 15 seconds for the backend to warm its caches.
  • Open Home, Finder & Map, Dishes and three or four centre pages once, so centre photos and map tiles are cached.
  • In Finder & Map, note one centre whose card shows an orange "Closes in N days" pill (for step B5) and one stall with a NEW grade (for step A7). They change over time, so pick them on the day.
  • Turn off notifications; set the browser zoom so text is readable from the back of the room; close other tabs.

Five minutes before

  • Sign out of the demo account, open http://localhost:5173, light theme, normal text size.
  • Have the use case diagram (lab3/1_use_case_model) open for the opening.

5. If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
Google sign-in fails or is slowSwitch to the backup browser profile that is already signed in, and continue from B3 (say the profile was set up earlier).
A page shows "Request failed" or loads very slowlyThe backend has stopped or Supabase is unreachable. Check the backend terminal; restart it. If Supabase is down, set DATABASE_URL=sqlite:///./cleancravings.db in backend/.env (a local copy of the real data) and restart; sign in with the dev sign-in.
"Use my location" is blockedType the postal code instead.
No centre is closing within 7 daysSkip the live alert in B5; show the Alerts page of the backup profile, or explain the rule using a centre's Upcoming closures.
No internet at allPlay the recorded demo video (section 7) and do parts D and E from the slides and IDE.

6. What not to demo or claim

  • Not built yet (say they're planned if asked): in-app routing (UC-2.1; we hand over to Google Maps or OneMap), the weekly health digest (UC-4.1), exporting a health summary (UC-4.2), stall-to-dish reports (UC-9.0), public ratings and comments (UC-11.0; visit ratings are private), voice search.
  • Data no open dataset provides: operating hours, prices, a stall's own dishes, SFA demerit points (the stall page says "not published"). Don't suggest we have them.
  • Health claims: say "suits your profile" or "within HPB guidance", never "healthy" or "safe to eat". Ratings are for typical recipes, not a particular stall.
  • Hygiene: a SAFE grade is SFA's assessment of a licensee's track record, not a guarantee.
